# Open recipe library

3,745 text-only community recipes are bundled in `packages/core/src/data/open-recipes.json` and browsable at `/discover/open`, linked from Discover. They are not inserted into tenant databases or misattributed to Savortome Kitchen. Saving editable copies into the private library is not implemented in this release; attribution must survive that future flow.

Source: [gossminn Wikibooks Cookbook dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gossminn/wikibooks-cookbook), scraped July 31, 2024. Its dataset card declares CC BY-SA 4.0. The pinned mini JSON has SHA256 `67fd704a6d9280742d2f217ad9f9e4f50921b84fcdefdff9bac749a518c4d46b`. Each recipe retains its original Wikibooks URL, contributor attribution, license URL and modification notice. Recipe content is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, independently of application code. See LICENSE.txt and [Wikibooks reuse policy](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Wikibooks:Copyrights). Images have separate licensing and are excluded.

Build: download that exact source to an ignored local directory, then run `python scripts/build-open-recipe-library.py SOURCE OUTPUT_DIRECTORY`. Move the generated recipes.json to the bundled source path. The importer refuses changed source bytes and deterministically records its output hash. Of 3,895 source pages, 150 lacked sufficient ingredient/procedure content and were excluded. URL, duplicate content, text and size checks also run. Only explicit ordered procedure sections become steps; no AI generates or rewrites instructions. Timing/servings remain unparsed source metadata, not trusted filters. HTML is never executed.

This is structural validation, not culinary, allergen or food-safety approval. The corpus is a historical snapshot and community quality varies. Do not label these recipes verified, invent photos/timing/nutrition, or treat catalogue availability as hosted deployment evidence. Local browsing does not require an external recipe API or paid provider.
